Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Pocket Poker is a single-player video poker game. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

Pocket Poker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your credit balance for each game mode
  • Your sound on/off preference
  • Your selected card deck
  • Which card decks you have unlocked

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

In-App Purchases

Pocket Poker offers optional one-time purchases for card deck themes. All transactions are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to any payment information.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Pocket Poker does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@cibsdevelopment.com.
